## Why it matters

- Privacy earns and retains trust of users, partners, vendors, and employees
- Violations are expensive (regulatory fines; contractual damages)
- Everyone shares responsibility — “see something, say something”

## Definitions to apply daily

- **Data subject** — any natural person
- **Personal data** — anything that identifies or can be linked to a person (including public handles)
- **Processing** — any operation, including mere storage/backup
- **Breach** — unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, alteration, destruction, integrity failure

## Privacy by design — six principles

1. Proactive & preventative  
2. Privacy as default  
3. Embedded into design  
4. Full functionality without dropping privacy  
5. End-to-end lifecycle security  
6. Visibility & transparency  

## Privacy risk

Legal/regulatory exposure **plus** breaking privacy promises to customers or regulators.

## Regulated enterprise data (e.g. PHI)

- Prefer **zero data retention**
- Contractual limits (BAA-style) on use/disclosure
- Safeguards, breach notification timelines, subcontractor flow-down
- Return/destroy at end of engagement
- Non-default retention → legal review first

## Privacy review funnel

Ask: lawful/fair/transparent? purpose-limited? minimized? accurate? retention-bounded? confidential?  
High residual risk → DPIA before launch.

## Security essentials

- Strong passwords + MFA  
- Prompt updates  
- Phishing vigilance (8 checks: sender, urgency, greeting, grammar, attachments, password asks, URLs, missing signature)  
- No moving support to random third-party messengers for “account recovery”  
- Report insider-threat concerns and suspicious solicitation  
- Think like an owner; report vulnerabilities and compromises immediately  

## Software & gen-AI hygiene

- Approved software inventory only (or explicit exception)  
- No confidential data in unapproved cloud or external LLMs without exception  
- No harassment / prohibited content generation on work systems  
- Project code stays in official project remotes  

## Travel & workplace safety

- Report international travel when required; follow high-risk device rules  
- Zero tolerance for threats/violence; report early; call emergency services if in danger
