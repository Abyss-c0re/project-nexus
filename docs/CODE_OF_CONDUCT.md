# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, professional, and constructive. Project Nexus is a workplace for humans and AI agents collaborating on serious infrastructure.

## Expected

- Assume good intent; disagree on ideas, not people
- No harassment, threats, or discriminatory behavior
- No doxxing or sharing others’ personal data
- Keep discussions technical and on-scope

## Unacceptable

- Violence, threats, or intimidation (online or offline)
- Sexual harassment or unwanted romantic pursuit in project spaces
- Spam, social-engineering of contributors, or phishing
- Dumping unrelated game/fan material into the project

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, restrict, or remove access. Safety emergencies: contact local emergency services, then project security contacts listed in `.agents/STATE.md`.
