# Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy is more than a checkbox. It is how Project Nexus earns and keeps trust with **users, partners, vendors, and contributors**.

## Core definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Data subject** | A natural person (user, employee, partner contact, contributor) |
| **Personal data** | Any information that can directly or indirectly identify a person |
| **Processing** | Any operation on personal data — including storage and backups |
| **Breach** | Unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, destruction, loss, or integrity failure |

Personal data includes handles, emails, phone numbers, real names, internal IDs that map to those, device IDs, IP addresses, cookie IDs, precise location, and **public posts tied to an identity**. If it can be linked to a person with high confidence, treat it as personal data.

## Privacy by design (six principles)

1. **Proactive & preventative** — ask privacy questions at design time
2. **Privacy as default** — protective controls without requiring user heroics
3. **Privacy embedded into design** — not bolted on after ship
4. **Full functionality** — meet product goals without dropping privacy standards
5. **End-to-end lifecycle protection** — from collection through deletion
6. **Visibility & transparency** — people understand what is collected and why

## Privacy risk

Privacy risk includes:

1. Potential concerns about personal data and legal obligations, and
2. Violations of privacy promises made to customers or regulators — even when those promises exceed the letter of a statute

Consequences include eroded trust, financial sanctions, and reputational harm.

## Reviews before launch

When a feature processes personal data, complete a **privacy review** answering:

- Lawful, fair, transparent processing?
- Specific, legitimate purpose only?
- Data minimization?
- Accuracy controls?
- Retention not longer than needed?
- Integrity and confidentiality protected?

High-risk processing may require a **Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)**. Do not launch until required assessments are finished.

## Zero unnecessary retention

Default stance: **do not retain personal or customer data** unless a documented purpose, retention period, and access control exist. Prefer ephemeral processing and hashed/anonymized analytics where possible.

## See something, say something

If you observe a potential privacy incident or breach, report it immediately through the project security path. Early reporting prevents silent failures.
